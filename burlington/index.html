Vermont has one bankruptcy district covering the entire state. All Burlington-area cases are filed here.

Chapter 7 vs. Chapter 13

Chapter 7 - Fresh Start

  • Discharge in 3-4 months
  • Eliminates credit cards, medical debt
  • Must pass the means test
  • May lose non-exempt property
  • Filing fee: $338
Learn more about Chapter 7 →

Chapter 13 - Payment Plan

  • 3-5 year repayment plan
  • Keep your home and car
  • Catch up on missed payments
  • No means test requirement
  • Filing fee: $313

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I file bankruptcy in Burlington?

Burlington residents file at the U.S. Bankruptcy Court at 11 Elmwood Ave, Burlington, VT 05401. Vermont has one federal bankruptcy district (D. Vt.). You will need to complete credit counseling before filing. More FAQs.

What exemptions apply in Burlington?

Vermont offers a $125,000 homestead exemption, $2,500 vehicle exemption, and a $400 + $7,000 wildcard. Filers can choose state or federal exemptions. Full exemptions.

How much does bankruptcy cost in Burlington?

Chapter 7 filing fee is $338 and Chapter 13 is $313. Attorney fees typically range from $1,200-$2,000 for Chapter 7 and $3,000-$4,500 for Chapter 13. Full cost breakdown.
