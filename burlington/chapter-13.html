Chapter 13 Bankruptcy in Burlington

Chapter 13 lets you keep your property while repaying debts over 3-5 years through a court-approved plan.

What Is Chapter 13?

Chapter 13 allows you to keep your property while repaying some or all debts over a 3- to 5-year repayment plan. No means test income limit, but you must have regular income. Filing fee: $313.

Who Should Consider Chapter 13?

  • Homeowners behind on mortgage payments
  • Filers whose income is too high for Chapter 7
  • People wanting to protect non-exempt property
  • Those with tax debt or car loan arrears
  • Anyone who received a Chapter 7 discharge within the past 8 years

Saving Your Home

Chapter 13 lets you cure mortgage arrears over the plan while resuming regular payments. The automatic stay stops foreclosure immediately. Vermont's $125,000 homestead exemption protects your equity.

Chapter 13 Timeline

  1. Credit counseling - Required before filing
  2. File petition and plan - Automatic stay takes effect
  3. 341 meeting - About 30 days after filing
  4. Confirmation hearing - 30-45 days after 341
  5. Plan payments - 3 to 5 years
  6. Discharge - After completing all plan payments

Frequently Asked Questions

What if I cannot make payments?

You can request a plan modification, a hardship discharge, or convert to Chapter 7.

Can I keep my car?

Yes. Chapter 13 lets you catch up on missed payments and may reduce the loan balance (cramdown) if you have owned it over 910 days.
